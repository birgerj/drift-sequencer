# Piano sample packs

Drift downloads these packs on demand when you pick one on Drift Synth Piano.
Built by `scripts/pianos/build-pianos.mjs`.

The samples keep their own licenses, listed per pack. Music you make with
them is yours.

## Concert Grand (`grand/`)

- Yamaha C5 grand (Salamander Grand Piano)
- Source: Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm, sfz by sfzinstruments (github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano)
- License: [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)
- Changes: two velocity layers selected, mixed to mono, resampled to 32 kHz, trimmed with a fade-out and normalised.

## Steinway (`steinway/`)

- Steinway grand, warm and round (Splendid Grand Piano)
- Source: Splendid Grand Piano, Akai samples, sfz by sfzinstruments (github.com/sfzinstruments/SplendidGrandPiano)
- License: [Public domain](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SplendidGrandPiano)
