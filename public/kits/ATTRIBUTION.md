# Sampled drum kits

Drift downloads these kits on demand when you pick one with the *Samples* knob
on Drift Synth Drums. Built by `scripts/kits/build-kits.mjs`.

The samples keep their own licenses, listed per kit. Kits under CC BY-SA are
shared under the same license as modified sample sets. Music you make with
them is yours.

## Acoustic (`acoustic/`)

- Source: AVL Drumkits by Glen MacArthur (bandshed.net/avldrumkits)
- License: [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/)
- Changes: selected hits, mixed to mono, trimmed and normalised.
